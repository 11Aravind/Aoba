Authentic Agricultural Lineage

Where the story begins.

Every AOBA pouch traces an unbroken line back to the misty estates of Kerala — the soil, the monsoon, the hands that tend it.

Stage 01
Farm

Old-growth pepper vines and organic groves climb the slopes of the Western Ghats, rooted in mineral-rich volcanic red soil.

Stage 02
Harvest

Every berry is picked by hand at the exact moment its sugars and aromatic oils reach their peak.

Stage 03
Select

Aroma evaluation and careful sorting for size, colour and moisture. Only the top grade moves forward.

Stage 04
Process

Traditional sun-curing on woven bamboo mats, then slow stone-cooling — locking in the volatile oils.

Stage 05
Pack

Nitrogen-flushed, triple-barrier pouches with UV and moisture protection — the aroma stays exactly as the estate intended.

Stage 06
Your Kitchen

Tear it open and the estate fills your room — pure, aromatic, unmistakably Kerala.
